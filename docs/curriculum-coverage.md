# Coverage of the Ministry of Education curriculum

Checked on 5 November 2026 against the documents that could be opened. Full lessons come from `docs/new-topics.md`, which is written so that these are covered by 60 lessons per course.

## Covered or scheduled
- Math, grades 2 to 7: operations, fractions, decimals, percent, ratio, price, rounding, time, scale, areas, volume, surface, data, angles, signed numbers, powers, roots, expressions, equations.
- Science: materials, energy, living world, human body, ecology, Earth and space, technology, inquiry.
- Geography, grades 5 to 9: maps, Mediterranean, Israel and Jerusalem, global issues, Earth sciences, Middle East.
- Homeland, society and citizenship, grades 2 to 4; history from the return to Zion to the 1950s.
- Hebrew: reading and grammar. Bible: books by grade, secular and religious lists.

## Not covered, and why
- Holocaust: awaits parent approval.
- Open writing, speaking and projects: the app has only closed questions.
- English grammar, listening and reading: the bank covers words, not all five domains.
- Literature, art and physical education: curricula not compared in detail.
- Grade-level detail not retrieved: history chapter lists, full math grade 4 to 6 programs, elementary Hebrew, elementary science list.
- Bible and heritage for secular state schools: not checked separately.
